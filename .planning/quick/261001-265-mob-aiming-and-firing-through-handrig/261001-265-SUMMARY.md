## Notes for the user

- Assign `eye` to a head bone on mob prefabs. Unset, it falls back to the mob's pivot; if that sits at the feet the sight ray can graze the floor and HandRig's look origin sits low.
- sightMask defaults to every raycast layer, so other mobs block sight (no shooting through allies). The HandRig cone can still lock onto an ally's limbs near the line until HandRig.ScorePart's TODO(human) weighs team; ownerLayerWeights can prefer the player's layer.
- Mob accuracy is tuned on the gun only (swayRadius, onTargetAccuracy, offTargetLooseness, idealFollowSpeed), plus the mob HandRig's maxAimCorrectionAngle and look-bloom gun fields, since body turning counts as look rate.
- Releasing a charge gun fires its charged shot, including on disengage/death releases.
- Semi-auto guns fire once per burst; shorten burstCooldown for those mobs if they feel slow.
- Mobs already in scenes now look from their own eye rather than Camera.main.
