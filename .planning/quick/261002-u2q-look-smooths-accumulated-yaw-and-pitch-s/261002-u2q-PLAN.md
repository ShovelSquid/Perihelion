<objective>
Fix the look snap-back in `Assets/Scripts/Look.cs`. A fast flick that gets the look target more than 180 degrees ahead of the lagging swivel currently turns the wrong way, and a full 360 ahead doesn't move at all. Store yaw and pitch as accumulated float angles, ease them with a frame-rate independent exponential lerp, and rebuild the swivel rotation from those angles each frame.

Purpose: a rotation quaternion has no winding count, and easing between two of them always takes the shortest arc. Any target more than half a turn ahead is therefore reached by turning backwards, and a whole turn ahead looks identical to where the swivel already is. Accumulated angles keep the winding, so the swivel follows the input all the way round.
Output: `Assets/Scripts/Look.cs` with four private float angle fields, a new Start initialisation, a rewritten LateUpdate (input, then ease or snap, then a yaw rebase, then the Euler rotation, then the position follow), and an updated lookLerpSpeed field comment. The public API is unchanged.
</objective>
