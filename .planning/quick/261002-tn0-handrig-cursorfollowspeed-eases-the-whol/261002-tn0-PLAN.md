Add one HandRig tunable, `cursorFollowSpeed`, that eases where the aim cursor's FRAME (the prongs) is drawn toward the hand's ideal aim point. A lock-on snap then glides on screen instead of popping. The DOT is not smoothed at all. It stays exactly on the true real aim point, where the muzzle aims and shots go.

Purpose: lock-on target switches currently teleport the prong frame from one point to another. Easing only the display frame keeps that motion readable without making aim, shots or the dot any less exact.
