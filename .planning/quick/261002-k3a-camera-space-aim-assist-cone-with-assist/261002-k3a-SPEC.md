# Spec: camera-space aim assist cone with assistAngle (agreed with user, 2026-10-02)

Follow-up to quick tasks 260930-x5h (per-hand ideal/real aim points) and 261001-14s (AimPart registry).

## Problem
Aim assist only locks when the crosshair is directly over a collider.
- The cone half-angle is exactly `slot.bloom`, which rests at `Gun.baseSpread` (0.5 deg default, 0.1 on some UPRISING guns): a few cm wide at 20 m.
- The cone is measured from the muzzle toward `lookReference`. Off-target, `AimInput` parks the aim point 1000 m out, so the axis runs parallel to the camera ray but offset by the gun's position. A target inside the screen circle is several degrees off the muzzle axis. Hovering it snaps `aimDistance` to its depth, which is the only time the axis passes through it.

## Decisions (LOCKED)
- Selection is measured from the eye: cone origin = `lookSource.position`, axis = toward `lookReference` (the smoothed look direction at the eased aim depth). With no `lookSource`, fall back to the shot origin (old behaviour). Mobs already set `lookSource` to their eye (`MobBrain`), so they get the same model.
- Cone half-angle = `slot.bloom + item.assistAngle`. New `Item.assistAngle` (degrees, default 2, clamped at >= 0) in the `[Header("Aim")]` block. Bloom keeps meaning shot spread, so the cursor prongs are unchanged.
- Validity stays at the muzzle: line of sight is cast from the shot origin (last frame's aimed muzzle), and `ScorePart`'s `distance` stays meters from the shot origin.
- The candidate's point is the part's point nearest the eye-ray, so the lock lands where the crosshair is closest to the part.
- `ScorePart` stays the single `TODO(human)` with its `return angleOffCentre` stub. Only its doc comment changes: `angleOffCentre` is now degrees off the eye's look axis, and `coneAngle` is the assist cone half-angle (bloom + assistAngle).
- No AimCursor change in this task. An assist ring showing the selection cone is a possible follow-up.
