# Proposed for Design

Derives from: (none, proposed)
Status: draft
Phase: 01

Each H2 below is one vault note that `Design/` does not have yet but the strategy sim needs; the body under it is written in the vault's own one-line voice so it can go in unchanged. Copy the body line into a new file at the path on the `Proposed path:` line (or, for the amendment, append it to the existing file) and the wikilinks between these notes and the vault resolve on their own.

The sections sit in the order the sim meets them: the vehicle, the job it carries, the people who do the job, the places the job starts and ends, and the message that says it is done.

## Shuttle (amendment)

Proposed path: `Design/Structure/Unit/Transport/Shuttle.md` (append to the existing note)

Makes a fixed round trip for every job it carries: loads at its home, flies out, unloads, waits on site while the job runs, and flies back.

## Mission

Proposed path: `Design/Mission.md`

A timed job staged from a [[Base]], carried to a [[Launch Site]] by a [[Shuttle]], run there by a [[Pilot]] in a [[Mech]], and ended with consequences for the hangar, the stockpiles and the [[Map]].

## Pilot

Proposed path: `Design/Character/Pilot.md`

A [[Character]] who flies a [[Mech]] on a [[Mission]], carries skill and fatigue between missions, and is either ready, assigned, in transit, in the field, recovering, or lost.

## Worker

Proposed path: `Design/Character/Worker.md`

A [[Character]] who keeps the [[Base]] running by working timed jobs in order of priority; the vault's Mechanic, who repairs and refits mechs in the [[Hangar]], is the first worker role.

## Hangar

Proposed path: `Design/Structure/Hangar.md`

The building at a [[Base]] that holds its mechs in bays, the spare parts and the workers who repair them, and where a [[Mission]] is staged before a [[Shuttle]] carries it out.

## Launch Site

Proposed path: `Design/Launch Site.md`

A point on the [[Map]] a [[Shuttle]] can land at, reached from a [[Base]] along a route with a known travel time, where a [[Mission]] is run and which the factions present there contest.

## Base

Proposed path: `Design/Structure/Base.md`

A site on the [[Map]] the player owns, holding a [[Hangar]] and a [[Depot]], from which shuttles leave with staged missions and to which they return with the consequences.

## Alert

Proposed path: `Design/Alert.md`

A message the world raises when something finishes or runs short (a [[Shuttle]] arrives, a [[Mission]] ends, a repair completes, a stockpile runs low) that the player may answer or ignore without stopping time.
